# Mathshelf

Games, videos and more for maths, for Years 7 to 10.

Open it at **https://hamzaashour949.github.io/mathshelf/**

Every resource says who is behind it:

- **Mr. Hamza's pick**: videos and other material he found, checked and picked for his students.
- **Made by Hamza Ashour**: games and tools he designed and built himself.

The site is in English and in Arabic. Lesson names and game names stay in English in both.

## On the shelf now

- **Year 7**: videos for Unit 2 (divisibility, factors and primes, negative numbers, squares and roots, calculations).
- **Year 8**: videos for Unit 2 (one-step, two-step and more complex equations, formulae), and the game Jelly Terms (ten stages of collecting like terms, with locked brackets and a hard mode).
- **Year 9**: videos for Unit 1 and Unit 2, and the game Pythagorean Theorem (drag the small squares into the big square on the hypotenuse; idea by Ms. Nour Kaware).
- **Year 10**: videos for Unit 2 (real numbers, exponents, functions, inverses, special functions, transformations).

Games open full screen, with a Back button.

Cognito is the first choice for every video. Other channels are used only where Cognito has no video that covers the lesson.
