# Mathshelf

Games, videos and more for maths, for Years 7 to 10.

Open it at **https://hamzaashour949.github.io/mathshelf/**

Every resource says who is behind it:

- **Mr. Hamza's pick**: videos and other material he found, checked and picked for his students.
- **Made by Hamza Ashour**: games and tools he designed and built himself.

The site is in English and in Arabic. Lesson names and game names stay in English in both.

## On the shelf now

**Year 9**

- Videos for Unit 1: 1.1 STEM: Powers of 10, 1.2 Calculating and estimating, 1.3 Indices, 1.4 Standard form.
- Game: Pythagorean Theorem. Drag the small squares into the big square on the hypotenuse. Idea by Ms. Nour Kaware.

Years 7, 8 and 10 have their pages ready and will fill up as resources are shared.
